Cook your family's favorite foods on a freestanding gas range that has two SpeedHeat™ Burners to sear and boil quickly, and Edge-to-Edge Cast-Iron Grates that are dishwasher safe. Get the flexibility to cook a wide variety of meals with help from Frozen Bake™ Technology, the built-in Broiler Drawer and more cooking features.

  • Sear and boil quickly with two SPEEDHEAT Burners

  • Skip preheating with FROZEN BAKE Technology

  • Cast-iron grates are dishwasher safe for easy cleaning

  • Get more space to sauté, boil and simmer with cast-iron grates that extend across the entire cooktop, giving you the flexibility to place pots and pans anywhere

  • Use the high heat of the Broiler Drawer to quickly melt or brown your favorite dishes in a separate cavity while you cook another dish in the main oven

  • Make pancakes, grill vegetables or sear steaks with the Optional Griddle that lets you optimize your cooktop space for breakfast, lunch and dinner (Optional Griddle sold separately)

  • Easily fit griddles and long cookware on this fifth burner, designed to effectively heat large surfaces

  • Easily cook large meals with a 5.1-cu ft Capacity oven that includes two racks and a separate Broiler Drawer

  • Disable the control panel to avoid unintended use and easily clean the controls
