## Usage Tips

### Focus on High-Traffic Areas
The stores in **Broward County** (7 stores) and **Palm Beach County** (6 stores) tend to have the highest inventory turnover and deal frequency.

### Weekend vs. Weekday
- **Weekends**: Focus on Miami-Dade and southern Broward (more residential shoppers)
- **Weekdays**: Focus on Palm Beach and northern Broward (contractor traffic)

### Seasonal Considerations
- **Hurricane Season** (June-Nov): Increased deals on generators, plywood, supplies
- **Winter** (Dec-Feb): Snowbird season - higher traffic in Palm Beach stores
- **Summer** (June-Aug): AC units, fans, outdoor equipment deals
