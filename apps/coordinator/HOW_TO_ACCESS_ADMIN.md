## 🔄 Switching Between Regions

### To Switch to Florida (Southeast):
1. Login to admin
2. **Uncheck** Washington and Oregon
3. **Check** Florida
4. Click "Save Configuration"
5. ✅ Workers now scrape Florida stores

### To Switch Back to Northwest:
1. Login to admin
2. **Uncheck** Florida
3. **Check** Washington and Oregon
4. Click "Save Configuration"
5. ✅ Workers now scrape WA/OR stores

### To Scrape Both Regions:
1. Login to admin
2. **Check** all three states (WA, OR, FL)
3. Click "Save Configuration"
4. ✅ Workers scrape all regions
